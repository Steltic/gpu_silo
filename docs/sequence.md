# Scene guide

[Play the chaptered movie](../../../releases/latest/download/GPU_Silo.mp4). Times are rounded to the nearest second. The movie chapters use the animation frame clock. A design basis strip with the rack count, pod count, heat load, cooler capacity and store volume stays at the bottom of every 3D scene. The plan view has a voice-over. The rest of the film is silent.

| Start | What to watch |
| ---: | --- |
| 0:00 | The concrete forms jump together in ten 3.2 m lifts. The pod sleeves are cast into the inner wall up to the top pod level. The cooler-frame anchors and conduits are cast into the outer wall. The panel counts the fixings as they are set. |
| 0:25 | Water-glycol starts filling the wall gap. Two crawlers lower the steel pod cage into the 17 m bore. A walkway ring at each level joins the shaft to the pod roots. From here the concrete is fully see-through and only its edges are drawn. |
| 0:40 | The water-glycol rises to 31.75 m. The panel gives the volume, the target and the heat buffer. |
| 0:47 | A flatbed delivers three 4SU pods. Each has the 5 m bronze trussed housing and a solid light-grey CDU and pump section at the wall end. Ten screened cooler frames stand in laydown round the silo. |
| 1:01 | The first 4SU travels through the ground opening, attaches to the hoist, spirals up and docks. The inset shows its coupling: the sleeve stops in the store and power arrives down the shaft. |
| 1:09 | While pods keep loading, the south crawler picks cooler frame 01 and seats it on its cast-in anchors at RL 13.2 to 21.6 m. The inset shows one 2 MW cooler, 12 m long. |
| 1:13 | Two fitters in the boom lift connect the supply and return spools to the cast-in conduits, row by row. |
| 1:16 | The crawler tracks anticlockwise round the silo and the boom lift follows. Frames alternate between the lower tier and the upper tier, RL 22.2 to 30.6 m. |
| 1:30 | After the top two levels, the remaining six levels of pods fill at triple speed. The tenth frame is connected as the last pods dock. |
| 1:52 | The backup diesel level comes in through the open top: four 2.5 MW sets and two day tanks on one deck. |
| 2:04 | The turbine level follows: two in-line 25 MW gas turbine generator sets side by side. |
| 2:12 | The crawler lowers the lid. It carries the two turbine exhaust stacks, the two air inlet houses, the diesel exhausts and the diesel room vents. The crawler then drives off. |
| 2:19 | The lid is hidden so the inside can be seen. Staff ride the wedge platforms to their work levels. One deck rises past the diesel level to the turbine level. |
| 2:36 | The outer side of turbine set A folds down to floor level and shows the gas turbine, its exhaust collector and its generator. The crew step from their deck onto the folded side and work on the turbine. |
| 2:44 | The crew step back, their deck starts down and the side closes as the camera leaves. |
| 2:50 | The concrete turns solid and the lid returns. The camera makes a full orbit of the finished silo while the panel lists the design basis. |
| 3:12 | Eight propane tanks and one diesel tank are set down 66 m from the silo. The propane line and the smaller diesel line run along the ground, up the outside wall, across the lid and down to the power levels. The panel gives the stored volumes and the run times. |
| 3:29 | Plan view, narrated. Hot water-glycol leaves each CDU through the inner wall and disperses into the store. Each cooler pump draws from the store, and the cooled liquid returns at the outer wall and disperses. Nothing is piped from a cooler to a pod. |
| 4:04 | Section. Power runs from the turbine sets down the steel shaft and out to every 4SU. Cold water-glycol passes over the racks and returns hot. The coolers draw hot and return cold. Propane runs from the tanks up the wall to the turbines, and diesel follows it to the backup sets. |
| 4:30 | The replacement scene, a cutaway again. A fault is flagged on 4SU 021, Level 6. Neighbouring pods clear the vertical lane. |
| 4:44 | The camera pulls back. While the faulty pod comes out through the door, the crawler returns and lowers cooler frame 09 to the ground. |
| 4:50 | One 2 MW cooler is drawn out of the frame and set down, and a replacement is lifted into its bay. |
| 4:57 | The replacement 4SU enters at grade. The crawler hangs the frame on its seats and the pipe spools are remade. |
| 5:18 | All 64 service positions reconnect. |
| 5:21 | The concrete turns solid for a last view of the finished silo. |
| 5:27 | End. |
