# GPU Silo film chapters

The film opens over the rural site, follows the construction programme and isolates the shaft, pod, cooler and turbine deck using geometry from the facility IFC. A vertical section traces the rack and cooler circuits through the thermal store. The closing orbit begins at 2:38 and runs for 22.5 seconds.
