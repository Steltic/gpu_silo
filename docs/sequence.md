# Scene guides

There are two films. [silo_short](#the-build-silo_short) is the build in programme order. [GPU_Silo](#the-full-presentation-gpu_silo) is the longer presentation. They share one model and one design file and are not kept in step.

## The full presentation: GPU_Silo

This film was made before the build order was revised. It fills the store before the pods and has no cage build, liner or site fabrication, so its day tags, which come from the current programme, do not run in order.

[Play the chaptered movie](../../../releases/latest/download/GPU_Silo.mp4). Times are rounded to the nearest second. The movie chapters use the animation frame clock. A design basis strip with the rack count, pod count, heat load, cooler capacity and store volume stays at the bottom of every 3D scene. A tag at the top of each scene gives its days in the 28-day programme and a running truck count. The plan view has a voice-over. The rest of the film is silent.

Day 0 is the day the last wall lift is poured. The film shows a sample of the trucks, and the counters scale to the full number of loads.

| Start | Programme | What to watch |
| ---: | --- | --- |
| 0:00 | Days -9 to 0 | The first lift at working speed. The forms carry a pour deck, a working platform, climbing rails and jacks, with starter bars standing above them. A placing boom on a mast at the silo axis turns once round the walls for each pour, fed by a pump at the ground opening. Truck mixers arrive round the haul ring and back up to the pump hopper two at a time. A crew of 22 rides the forms: placers and finishers during the pour, ironworkers on the bars, carpenters stripping and resetting the panels, jack operators on the jump. The panel lists the crew and the concrete in one lift. |
| 0:06 | | Nine more 3.2 m lifts, one a day, 14 mixers each. Trailing platforms hang below the forms from the second lift. The pod sleeves are cast into the inner wall up to the top pod level, and the cooler-frame anchors and conduits into the outer wall. |
| 0:25 | Day 3 | Two crawlers lower the steel pod cage into the 17 m bore. Behind the silo, water tankers start cycling through the three bays of the water drive and the level in the wall gap begins to rise. Frame steel and the first coolers arrive for frames 01 and 10: each truck stops in the stopping lane and the assist crane lifts the cooler into its frame. From here the concrete is fully see-through and only its edges are drawn. |
| 0:40 | Days 3 to 12 | The water-glycol rises to 31.75 m. The panel gives the volume, the target and the heat buffer, and the tag counts the 312 tanker loads. |
| 0:47 | Day 6 | The receiving dock. A truck backs into the far lane with two 4SU pods end to end, which is the most the weight limit allows. The gantry spans both lanes and lifts each pod onto a cradle on the rail. A second truck backs in as the first leaves. |
| 1:01 | Days 6 to 14 | The first 4SU travels through the ground opening, attaches to the hoist, spirals up and docks. The inset shows its coupling: the sleeve stops in the store and power arrives down the shaft. |
| 1:09 | | While pods keep loading, the south crawler picks cooler frame 01 and seats it on its cast-in anchors at RL 13.2 to 21.6 m. The inset shows one 2 MW cooler, 12 m long. |
| 1:13 | | Two fitters in the boom lift connect the supply and return spools to the cast-in conduits, row by row. |
| 1:16 | | The crawler tracks anticlockwise round the silo and the boom lift follows. Ahead of it the assist crane keeps loading coolers into the frames still in laydown. Pod trucks wait in the holding lanes, lap the ring to the dock and leave by the access road. |
| 1:30 | | After the top two levels, the remaining six levels of pods fill at triple speed, and the pod trucks with them. The tenth frame is connected as the last pods dock. The tag counts 32 pod trucks and 30 coolers. |
| 1:52 | Days 15 to 17 | The backup diesel level comes in through the open top: four 2.5 MW sets and two day tanks on one deck. |
| 2:04 | | The turbine level follows: two in-line 25 MW gas turbine generator sets side by side. |
| 2:12 | | The crawler lowers the lid. It carries the two turbine exhaust stacks, the two air inlet houses, the diesel exhausts and the diesel room vents. The crawler then drives off. |

| 3:27 | | Propane transports pull into the two-bay lay-by beside the tank header and unload. The panel gives the stored volumes and the run times, and the tag counts the 26 propane loads. |

| 4:11 | | Section. Power runs from the turbine sets down the steel shaft and out to every 4SU. Cold water-glycol passes over the racks and returns hot. The coolers draw hot and return cold. Propane runs from the tanks up the wall to the turbines, and diesel follows it to the backup sets. |
