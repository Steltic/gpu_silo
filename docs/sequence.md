# Scene guide

[Play the chaptered movie](../../../releases/latest/download/GPU_Silo.mp4). Times are rounded to the nearest second. The movie chapters use the animation frame clock. A design basis strip with the rack count, pod count, heat load, cooler capacity and store volume stays at the bottom of every 3D scene. The plan view has a voice-over. The rest of the film is silent.

| Start | What to watch |
| ---: | --- |
| 0:00 | The concrete forms jump together. The pod sleeves are cast into the inner wall up to the top pod level. The cooler-frame anchors and conduits are cast into the outer wall. The panel counts the fixings as they are set. |
| 0:25 | Water-glycol starts filling the wall gap. Two crawlers lower the steel pod cage into the 13 m bore. From here the concrete is fully see-through and only its edges are drawn. |
| 0:40 | The water-glycol rises to 29.75 m. The panel gives the volume, the target and the heat buffer. |
| 0:47 | A flatbed delivers three 4SU pods. Each has the 5 m bronze trussed housing and a solid light-grey CDU and pump section at the wall end. Eight screened cooler frames stand in laydown round the silo. |
| 1:01 | The first 4SU travels through the ground opening, attaches to the hoist, spirals up and docks. The inset shows its coupling: the sleeve stops in the store and power arrives down the shaft. |
| 1:09 | While pods keep loading, the south crawler picks cooler frame 01 and seats it on its cast-in anchors at RL 9.5 to 19.5 m. The inset shows one 1 MW cooler. |
| 1:13 | Two fitters in the boom lift connect the supply and return spools to the cast-in conduits, row by row. |
| 1:16 | The crawler tracks anticlockwise round the silo and the boom lift follows. Frames alternate between the lower tier and the upper tier, RL 20 to 30 m. |
| 1:30 | After the top two levels, the remaining six levels of pods fill at triple speed. The eighth frame is connected as the last pods dock. |
| 1:52 | Two power modules enter through the open top and seat at 90° on the shaft. |
| 2:12 | Four vents extend above the rim. |
| 2:15 | The crawler lowers the lid over the bore and the store. The vents stand through it. |
| 2:21 | The lid is hidden so the inside can be seen. Staff ride the wedge platforms to their work levels and the lower power module. |
| 2:54 | A fault is flagged on 4SU 021, Level 6. Neighbouring pods clear the vertical lane. |
| 3:08 | The camera pulls back. While the faulty pod comes out through the door, the crawler lowers cooler frame 07 to the ground. |
| 3:14 | One 1 MW cooler is drawn out of the frame and set down, and a replacement is lifted into its bay. |
| 3:21 | The replacement 4SU enters at grade. The crawler hangs the frame back on its seats and the pipe spools are remade. |
| 3:42 | All 64 service positions reconnect. |
| 3:45 | The concrete turns solid and the lid returns. The camera makes a full orbit of the finished silo while the panel lists the design basis. |
| 4:06 | Eight propane tanks are set down 60 m from the silo. The gas line runs along the ground, up the outside wall, across the lid and down to the power modules. The panel gives the stored volume and the run time. |
| 4:23 | Plan view, narrated. Hot water-glycol leaves each CDU through the inner wall and enters the store. Each cooler pump draws it out, and cold water-glycol enters the store at the outer wall. |
| 4:56 | Section. Power runs from the modules down the steel shaft and out to every 4SU. Cold water-glycol passes over the racks and returns hot. The coolers draw hot and return cold. Gas runs from the tanks up the wall to the modules. |
| 5:18 | End. |
