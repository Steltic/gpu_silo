# Scene guide

[Play the chaptered movie](../../../releases/latest/download/GPU_Silo.mp4). Times are rounded to the nearest second. The movie chapters use the animation frame clock. A design basis strip with the rack count, pod count, heat load, cooler capacity and store volume stays at the bottom of every scene.

| Start | What to watch |
| ---: | --- |
| 0:00 | The concrete forms jump together. The pod service sleeves, the cooler-frame anchors and conduits, and the watertight doorway are cast into the shell. The panel counts the fixings as they are set. |
| 0:25 | Water-glycol starts filling the wall gap. Two crawlers lower the steel pod cage into the 13 m bore. The concrete is drawn as glass so the cage can be followed. |
| 0:40 | The concrete is solid again. The south crawler has stayed. Eight dry cooler frames stand in laydown round the silo, each with four coolers and four pump units. The crawler picks the first frame and hoists it. |
| 0:44 | The first frame moves in to the wall and seats on its cast-in anchors at RL 20 to 30 m. The inset shows one 1 MW cooler. |
| 0:45 | Two fitters in the boom lift rise along the pump end of the frame and connect the supply and return spools to the cast-in conduits, row by row. |
| 0:48 | The crawler tracks round the silo to the next frame and the boom lift follows. Frames alternate between the lower tier, RL 9.5 to 19.5 m, and the upper tier. |
| 1:12 | All eight frames are connected. The concrete turns to glass and the water-glycol rises to 29.75 m. The panel gives the volume, the target and the heat buffer. |
| 1:19 | A flatbed delivers three 4SU pods. Each has the 5 m bronze trussed housing and a solid light-grey CDU and pump section at the wall end. |
| 1:33 | Each 4SU travels through the ground opening, attaches to the hoist, spirals up and docks. The cooler frames nearest the camera become translucent so the lift stays visible. |
| 2:02 | After the top two levels, the remaining six fill at triple speed. |
| 2:25 | Two power modules enter through the open top. They seat at 90° on the shaft and extend their exhausts to the rim. |
| 2:47 | Staff ride the wedge platforms to their work levels and the lower power module. |
| 3:20 | A fault is flagged on 4SU 021, Level 6. Neighbouring pods clear the vertical lane, the faulty pod leaves through the door and the replacement enters at grade. |
| 4:08 | All 64 service positions reconnect. |
| 4:11 | The concrete turns solid. The camera makes a full orbit of the finished silo while the panel lists the design basis. |
| 4:34 | End. |
