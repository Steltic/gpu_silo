# Design decisions

The movie shows the current design. [Project inputs](project.json) are versioned with the geometry, animation and checks, and the [calculations](calcs.md) and the [structural design report](report.md) are generated from the same file. A contributor can change one pod, the shared design or the way a scene is filmed. Each change has a reviewable diff, a recalculated design basis and a movie preview.

## System

256 NVIDIA Vera Rubin NVL72 racks in 64 pods of four, 195 kW of liquid heat each. Liquid heat load 50 MW.

| Side | Equipment | Path |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Heat collection | 64 CDUs, one per pod, each with its own VFD pump | Pod cold plates → CDU → wall coupling → store |
| Thermal store | Closed-loop water and propylene glycol between the two concrete walls | Decouples the two sides, buffers load and steadies cooler staging |
| Heat rejection | 30 dry coolers of 2 MW, each with its own pump, in 10 steel frames. 25 carry the load. | Store → cast-in conduit → pump → cooler → cast-in conduit → store |

Central control manages CDU pump speed, dry cooler fan speed and staging, and cooler pump staging against store temperature and total heat load.

## How dimensions propagate

The liquid gap is `(outside diameter − inside diameter) / 2 − outer skin − inner skin`. At 25 m and 17 m with 250 mm skins it is **3.50 m**. The pod root is at the shaft radius plus the access, **1.20 m**. The CDU and pump section is whatever length fills the pod out to the tip clearance, so the wall end stays at **8.36 m** when the access changes. The corner of the 0.76 m wide frame sweeps a radius of **8.369 m**, which leaves **131 mm** to the 8.50 m bore.

A cooler frame is flat and the wall is curved. Its back plane stands 0.20 m off the wall at its centre and 2.02 m off at each end. The anchor struts bridge that gap.

Unit numbers follow the installation order, starting at 001 on the top level, then round a level before moving down. The exchange spare has the next number. Cooler frames are numbered anticlockwise from the ground entry. Dimensions are in metres, angles in degrees and colours in sRGB hexadecimal.
